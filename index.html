iv. projects

A Control and Embedded Software System for a Bioinspired Hummingbird Flapping-Wing Micro Aerial Vehicle 2025–2026

ESP32-C3 firmware (ESP-IDF) and a Jetpack Compose BLE controller, with CRC-8 framing, three independent fail-safes, and a complementary-filter attitude estimator driving anti-windup PID. The command link and the control loop were demonstrated on the bench; the estimator runs against a simulated IMU, as no inertial sensor was integrated.

Advisor: Harun Artuner · Course: Capstone Design Project (BBM479/480) · Paper ↗ · Poster ↗ · Code ↗ · Demo ↗

workbench86 Spring 2026

A real-mode 8086 workbench for the DIGIAC 2000 lab trainer, in the browser: an assembler, a simulator whose debugger steps backwards as well as forwards, and a WebSerial bridge that uploads the assembled bytes to the physical board.

Course: Microprocessors (BBM436) · Open the lab ↗

RBMK-1000 Reactor Simulation Spring 2026

An interactive browser model of the Soviet reactor design behind Chernobyl, detailed enough to show why the accident happened: the positive void coefficient, xenon-135 poisoning, the graphite-tipped control rods, and the AZ-5 shutdown that briefly raised power instead of dropping it.
